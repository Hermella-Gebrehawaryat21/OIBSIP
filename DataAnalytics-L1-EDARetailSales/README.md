## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Oasis Infobyte Internship Program (OIBSIP), Data Analytics Track.

The objective is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, and product category performance.

## Objectives

- Inspect and understand the retail sales dataset.
- Calculate descriptive statistics for numerical variables.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly revenue trends.
- Explore customer demographics, including age groups and gender.
- Analyze product category performance.
- Examine correlations between numerical variables.
- Develop actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

## Key Findings

- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly revenue of 53,150.
- Q4 2023 recorded the highest quarterly revenue of 126,190.
- Customers aged 30–59 accounted for 63.4% of transaction records.
- Electronics generated the highest total revenue of 156,905.
- Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold, with 894 units.
- Price per Unit had a strong positive correlation with Total Amount (0.85).

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve inventory planning for Clothing based on its higher sales volume.
2. Monitor Electronics revenue and sales performance regularly.
3. Test targeted promotions to improve Beauty sales volume.
4. Use customer age-group insights to guide and evaluate marketing campaigns.
